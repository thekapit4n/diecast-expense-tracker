# Changes Log - 2026-07-31

This note records all work done today in simple language.

## Quick Overview

- Added a new "Facebook Bid" option to the Platform choice on the Add Purchase and Edit Purchase forms.
- Files touched: `app/purchase/add/page.tsx`, `components/purchase/edit-purchase-modal.tsx`.

## What Changed

### 1) New "Facebook Bid" platform option

- The Platform field (shown when Purchase Type is "Online") already had "Facebook Marketplace" for normal Facebook Marketplace listings.
- Some purchases don't work that way — a seller posts photos in a Facebook group or page, and buyers have to bid or claim the item in the comments of that post to seal the deal. That's a different buying experience worth tracking separately.
- Added a new option "Facebook Bid" (stored value: `facebook_bid`) right after "Facebook Marketplace" in both the Add Purchase form and the Edit Purchase form, so the two stay in sync.
- No database changes were needed — the `platform` column already stores free text, so the new option just adds another valid value.

## User Impact

- When adding or editing a purchase, choosing Purchase Type "Online" and then Platform now shows a "Facebook Bid" choice, separate from "Facebook Marketplace".
- This makes it possible to tell apart, in reports and filters later, purchases bought through normal Marketplace listings versus group posts where the price was settled by bidding in the comments.

## Notes for Next Time

- If more Facebook-group-style buying patterns show up (e.g. live bidding videos), consider whether they need their own platform value or fit under "Facebook Bid".
